📖 Unseen — מבחן 1 באנגלית

Read the text

Last summer, Maya and her family visited a small village near a wide river. On the first day, they met a local guide named Sam. He showed them an old bridge and told them about the history of the area. Maya was surprised to learn that people had used the bridge for more than one hundred years.

The next morning, the family joined a walking tour. The weather was warm, but the path was sometimes dangerous, so Sam asked everyone to be careful. During the walk, Maya saw unusual birds and took many photos. At lunchtime, the group stopped near the river and ate a simple meal together.

At the end of the trip, Maya said that the village was one of the most interesting places she had ever visited. She hoped to return there one day.
